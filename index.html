Dear Joe,

This is honestly one of the hardest farewell messages for me to write because I genuinely feel very sad that our time working together has come to an end so soon.

Thank you for being such an incredible boss, mentor, and more importantly, such a genuinely good person. One of the things I respect most about you is how you treat everyone with the same level of respect, regardless of position or background. You never needed to demand respect from people, you earned it through the way you treated others.

And somehow hor, you never fail to amuse me every now and then. 😂 Whether it’s your humour, cheekiness, random comments, or suddenly seeing you dismantle something because you decided it could be fixed or improved, there was always something going on.

Sometimes I really wonder what exactly you cannot do. Plumbing can, certified electrician also can, gardening also can, until you literally planted a tree along the roadside. 😂 Wherever you go, you somehow find something that can be repaired, improved, or made better. I think that says a lot about you too, you naturally leave things better than how you found them.

What impressed me even more was how people reacted to you wherever we went. Seeing the VVIP treatment you received in Suzhou, till this day still the most lavish business meal I ever had, thanks to you! How warmly your current and former colleagues greeted you, and the messages from people thanking you really showed the impact you’ve had throughout your career.

That kind of respect cannot fake one. It only comes from years of genuinely treating people well.

I’ve also always admired your patience and EQ. I honestly cannot remember seeing you truly lose your temper. Even when things get messy or frustrating, you somehow remain calm and handle things with a lot of patience.

I’m especially grateful that you gave me the trust and freedom to do my work, while still being there whenever I needed guidance. I never regretted having the opportunity to work under you. If anything, my only regret is that the time was too short, because I still feel there are so many things I haven’t learned from you yet.

I really feel very sayang that you have to go. Work will definitely feel different without you around, and I’ll miss the random conversations, jokes, stories, and little lessons along the way.

You have your own business, and I sincerely hope it continues to grow and blossom into something even bigger. After spending so many years improving other people’s businesses, maybe now it’s time to put all that experience and energy into building something truly yours.

And when the business huat already, please remember your former employee here. 😂 Hire me again o. I’m serious one, please keep one vacancy for me.

Thank you for everything, the trust, guidance, patience, opportunities, laughter, knowledge, and all the things you taught me, whether directly or indirectly.

I genuinely feel very fortunate that I had the chance to work with you.

Take good care of yourself, enjoy the next chapter, and I sincerely wish you and your business nothing but success.

And please don’t forget me when you become the big boss of your own empire. ❤️

Signed, Wen Xin
